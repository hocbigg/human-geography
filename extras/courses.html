Great Courses in Human Geography

This curated collection gathers exceptional university-level open courseware, comprehensive MOOCs, and recorded academic lecture series in Human Geography. These offerings build deep conceptual models in spatial theory, critical urbanism, political economy, environmental governance, and geospatial science.

  • Urbanization, Spatial Planning & Cities
  • Global Development, Geopolitics & Political Economy
  • Environmental Governance, Political Ecology & Climate Justice
  • Geospatial Analysis, Critical Cartography & GIS

Urbanization, Spatial Planning & Cities

  • Theory of City Form (MIT OpenCourseWare / Julian Beinart)
  • Rethink the City: New Approaches to Global and Local Urban Challenges (edX / Delft University of Technology)
  • Public Transportation Systems (MIT OpenCourseWare / Nigel Wilson)
  • Greening the Economy: Sustainable Cities (Coursera / Lund University)

Global Development, Geopolitics & Political Economy

  • Reading Marx's Capital with David Harvey (CUNY Graduate Center / David Harvey)
  • The Age of Sustainable Development (Coursera / Columbia University / Jeffrey Sachs)
  • Capitalism: Success, Crisis, and Reform (Open Yale Courses / Douglas Rae)

Environmental Governance, Political Ecology & Climate Justice

  • Environmental Justice (Coursera / University of Michigan)
  • Urban Nature: Connecting Cities, Sustainability and Innovation (Coursera / Lund University & Durham University)
  • Climate Change Mitigation in Developing Countries (Coursera / University of Cape Town)

Geospatial Analysis, Critical Cartography & GIS

  • Fundamentals of GIS (Coursera / UC Davis)
  • Cartography (Esri)
  • Geographic Information System (GIS) Tutorial (MIT OpenCourseWare)
  • A Course in Geographic Data Science (University of Liverpool / Dani Arribas-Bel)
