# Great Courses

This curated collection gathers exceptional university-level open courseware, comprehensive MOOCs, and recorded academic lecture series in Human Geography. These offerings build deep conceptual models in spatial theory, critical urbanism, political economy, environmental governance, and geospatial science.

- [Global Development, Geopolitics & Political Economy](#global-development-geopolitics--political-economy)

- [Geospatial Analysis, Critical Cartography & GIS](#geospatial-analysis-critical-cartography--gis)

## Urbanization, Spatial Planning & Cities

- **[Theory of City Form (MIT OpenCourseWare / Julian Beinart)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-241j-theory-of-city-form-spring-2013/)**
  A comprehensive graduate lecture series on the historical, social, and economic forces shaping urban morphology and physical city forms across cultures.

- **[Greening the Economy: Sustainable Cities (Coursera / Lund University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/sustainable-cities)**
  Explores sustainable urban transformations, municipal climate experiments, and circular urban infrastructure across European and global metropolitan areas.

- **[Public Transportation Systems (MIT OpenCourseWare / Nigel Wilson)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-258j-public-transportation-systems-spring-2017/)**
  Covers the planning, network design, spatial operations, and socio-economic dynamics of urban transit systems.

- **[Smart Cities: Management of Smart Urban Infrastructures (Coursera / École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/smart-cities)**
  Examines how legacy urban infrastructures (water, energy, mobility) are transformed through digital platforms, spatial governance, and sensor networks.

## Global Development, Geopolitics & Political Economy

- **[Reading Marx's Capital with David Harvey (CUNY Graduate Center / David Harvey)](https://davidharvey.org/reading-capital/)**
  The definitive open-access semester lecture series by one of geography's most influential scholars, presenting a spatial and political-economic reading of *Capital*.

- **[The Age of Sustainable Development (Coursera / Columbia University / Jeffrey Sachs)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/the-age-of-sustainable-development)**
  A wide-ranging survey of global economic development patterns, regional inequality, planetary boundaries, and transnational governance.

## Environmental Governance, Political Ecology & Climate Justice

- **[Urban Nature: Connecting Cities, Sustainability and Innovation (Coursera / Lund University & Durham University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/urban-nature)**
  Led by political ecologists and urban geographers to evaluate nature-based solutions, green gentrification, and ecological governance in cities.

- **[Global Environmental Management (Coursera / Technical University of Denmark)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/global-environmental-management)**
  Focuses on international environmental policy, global trend analysis, and regional ecosystem management frameworks.

- **[Climate Change Mitigation in Developing Countries (Coursera / University of Cape Town)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/climate-change-mitigation)**
  Analyzes energy transitions, development trade-offs, and socio-spatial equity in the Global South through empirical case studies.

## Geospatial Analysis, Critical Cartography & GIS

- **[Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Specialization (Coursera / UC Davis)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/gis)**
  A five-course sequence covering spatial data models, vector/raster analysis, cartographic design, and satellite remote sensing workflows.

- **[Cartography MOOC (Esri)](https://www.esri.com/training/catalog/596e584bb826875993ba4ebf/cartography/)**
  An advanced visual design masterclass taught by professional cartographers on typography, projection selection, multivariate symbology, and map ethics.

- **[Geographic Information System (GIS) Tutorial (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-str-001-geographic-information-system-gis-tutorial-january-iap-2022/)**
  A modular laboratory course introducing spatial data integration, urban land-use analysis, and demographic mapping.
