These advanced specialization tracks build directly on the core curriculum, allowing learners to master specialized theoretical frameworks, advanced spatial methodologies, and contemporary research frontiers.

- **Critical Geopolitics, Territoriality & Decolonial Spatialities**
  Interrogates statecraft, borders, carceral systems, and indigenous counter-cartographies beyond Eurocentric territorial models.

- **Political Ecology, Environmental Justice & Climate Futures**
  Investigates the power relations governing nature, environmental racism, urban metabolic flows, and community-scale climate adaptation.

- **Global Production, Uneven Development & Critical Urbanism**
  Analyzes the spatial logics of global commodity chains, post-development theory, planetary urbanization, and logistical infrastructure.

- **Social Geographies of Difference, Embodiment & Mobilities**
  Explores how spatial relations co-constitute gender, sexuality, race, disability, health disparities, and transnational migration.

- **Critical GIS, Spatial Humanities & Geographic Data Science**
  Bridges computational spatial data science and spatial humanities with critical scrutiny of algorithmic governance and cartographic power.
